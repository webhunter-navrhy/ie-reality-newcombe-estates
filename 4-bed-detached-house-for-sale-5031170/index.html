Nestled along the highly sought-after Porterstown Road, Oatlands Gate Lodge presents a truly exceptional opportunity to create a landmark home in one of Castleknock’s most desirable residential settings. Occupying approximately 0.13 hectares (c. one-third acre), this distinctive property combines heritage charm with contemporary architectural vision.

Formerly the original gate lodge to the historic Oatlands House estate, the existing two bedroomed property comes with the benefit of full planning permission on this protected structure for a stunning modern architect-designed 4 bedroom residence extending to approximately 163 square meters, thoughtfully incorporating the existing cottage into a bright and elegant contemporary home.

The approved design has been carefully conceived to maximise natural light and garden views, creating a seamless connection between indoor living space and the magnificent surrounding grounds. Spacious and well-balanced accommodation is designed for modern family living, with generous reception areas and bright open-plan spaces overlooking the landscaped gardens.

The approved design provides for a high-quality contemporary single-storey extension to the existing two bedroomed gate lodge. Designed by a respected Dublin architect, the proposed new residence uses a striking modern architectural language, with a black zinc roof and dark external cladding, creating a refined contrast with the historic character of the original lodge. The design allows the old and new elements to sit together as a distinctive residential package: a restored period gate lodge paired with a contemporary architectural extension.

The existing lodge measuring 64 square meters has undergone a comprehensive complete renovation, with works costing in excess of €100,000. The renovation was carried out with care and attention to the building’s heritage, combining modern comfort and performance with the period character of the original cottage.

Substantial upgrades were completed throughout. The floors were fully excavated and rebuilt, with a damp-proof membrane, radon barrier, six inches of insulation, underfloor heating and new screeds installed. The walls were treated appropriately for a building of this age, drylined and backed with protective felt to help manage moisture, while the ceilings were fully insulated. Airtightness membranes were also installed throughout the rooms, improving the comfort and performance of the building.

Internally, the finish is of a very high standard. All rooms have been freshly plastered and professionally completed, with bespoke parquet wood flooring installed in the hall and sitting room areas, and high-quality bespoke tiling fitted in the bathroom. The cottage has also been fully rewired, with a new electrical board installed to allow for future expansion, ensuring the property is well prepared for integration into the approved larger residence.

The property which is approached by secure electric gates overlooks beautifully maintained grounds featuring mature native trees, manicured lawns and an idyllic private setting rarely found so close to Dublin city. This is an extraordinary opportunity for purchasers seeking to create a bespoke family residence of distinction in an exclusive and highly convenient location.

Perfectly positioned, Oatlands Gate Lodge enjoys immediate access to a host of exceptional amenities including Castleknock Hotel, Castleknock Golf Club and the scenic Strawberry Beds. Castleknock Village, excellent schools, transport link, the Phoenix Park and the M50 are all within easy reach, making this one of Dublin 15’s most prestigious addresses.
